# Gotcha 2.0 — Global Career Operating System

Design principle: preserve the existing Gotcha visual language, layout, navigation density, typography, spacing, cards, colors and interaction patterns. New functionality must extend the existing experience rather than replace it.

## 20 Functional Product Pillars
1. AI Opportunity Engine — explainable match scoring across skills, experience, salary, location, remote and visa preferences.
2. Universal Professional Profile — one canonical career profile powering search, CVs, applications and AI.
3. CV Intelligence — ATS analysis, job-specific optimization, resume variants and cover letters.
4. Global Market Intelligence — role/country/city demand, salary, competition and skill trends.
5. Application Command Center — pipeline, contacts, reminders, documents, interviews and offers.
6. AI Career Agent — personalized opportunity discovery and next-best-action recommendations.
7. Hunt Mode — daily job-search missions, recommendations and Career Hunt Score.
8. Gotcha Signals — proactive hiring, expansion, funding and market opportunity signals.
9. Referral Network — referral discovery, requests, offers and trust/reputation.
10. Global Mobility — visa sponsorship, relocation, remote eligibility and country suitability.
11. Company Intelligence — company profiles, hiring trends, skills demand and opportunity signals.
12. Interview Intelligence — mock interviews, role-specific questions, STAR coaching and scoring.
13. Career Development Engine — skills-gap analysis and 30/60/90-day development plans.
14. Career Circles & Mentoring — focused professional communities, mentors and peer support.
15. Stealth & Privacy Mode — employer blocking, anonymous discovery and recruiter visibility controls.
16. Gotcha Shield — job/recruiter risk signals and scam detection.
17. Career Analytics — application, interview, offer, CV and search-performance metrics.
18. Unified Global Navigation — organize all capabilities without disrupting the existing shell.
19. Career Command Center — existing dashboard upgraded with actionable career intelligence.
20. Career Digital Twin — persistent AI representation of the professional's skills, goals, history and target trajectory.

## Delivery rules
- Existing design/layout remains the source of truth.
- No destructive redesign unless explicitly approved.
- Features must be functional, persisted where appropriate, and wired into existing state/data/auth architecture.
- Prefer incremental migrations and backwards-compatible routes.
- Validate with typecheck, lint, tests and production build before release.
